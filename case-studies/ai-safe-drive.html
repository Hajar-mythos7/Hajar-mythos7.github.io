CASE STUDY 03 · ADAS · COMPUTER VISION · EDGE

AI Safe Drive Guardian

A Morocco-localized ADAS prototype: drowsiness detection via EAR, phone-use detection with YOLOv8n at 37 FPS on CPU, night-driving enhancement and dalam — voice + SMS.

YOLOv8OpenCVDlibPythonTwilio

CONTENTS

01 · Problem 02 · Context 03 · Architecture 04 · Methodology 05 · Implementation 06 · Results 07 · Lessons learned 08 · Future improvements

01Problem

Driver drowsiness and phone use are leading causes of road accidents — yet many safety systems need expensive hardware. The goal: a camera-based ADAS that runs on a standard computer, works at night, and alerts in the driver's own language.

02Context

A 2026 project with a real constraint: no GPU, no proprietary SDK — pure Python + OpenCV + Dlib + YOLOv8 on CPU. Localization mattered: alerts in Moroccan darija and an SMS fallback for critical events.

03Architecture

DETECTION PIPELINE

Two parallel tracks feed one alert engine: drowsiness (landmarks → EAR) and phone use (YOLOv8 object detection).

04Methodology

  • Drowsiness: Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) from Dlib landmarks, with a per-sequence average to reject blinks.
  • Phone use: YOLOv8n fine-tuned/Pretrained detection of phone-in-hand while driving.
  • Night: CLAHE contrast enhancement before landmark detection.
  • Alerting: escalating alerts — visual + darija voice; critical events via Twilio SMS.

05Implementation

  • OpenCV capture loop; multi-threaded inference to keep the frame rate high.
  • EAR state machine: idle → suspected → confirmed, with cooldown.
  • moroccan darija audio prompts + Twilio SMS on confirmed critical cases.

06Results

91%EAR drowsiness detection
93%phone-use detection
37 FPSon CPU, no GPU

Both tracks stay real-time on a plain CPU, and the CLAHE step keeps drowsiness detection usable in low-light/night conditions.

07Lessons learned

  • Blink rejection is everything — raw EAR is noisy, state machines make it usable.
  • Localized alerts change adoption; a driver reacts to their own language.
  • Balancing two detectors means scheduling work, not dumping frames.

08Future improvements

  • Head-pose estimation for distraction and driver-identification.
  • Quantized models for embedded deployment (Pi / Jetson).
  • Dashcam recording and accident-triple telemetry for insurance.
